    Willow Table house rules

    • 136 tiles: Characters, Dots, Bamboo, winds, and dragons. Seats proceed East (you), South, West, North; East opens the hand.
    • Win with four sets (runs or triples) and one pair. No special hands, flowers, or bonus tiles.
    • On a discard, Ron may be claimed by anyone, Pon by anyone, and Chi only by the next player. Ron has priority, then Pon, then Chi.
    • No Kan, Riichi, betting, or scorekeeping. A hand ends on a win or when the wall is exhausted.
    • This hand saves automatically in this browser. Refreshing resumes it; New hand replaces it.
