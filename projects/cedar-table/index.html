    House rules and local opening

    Open locally: in this folder run python3 -m http.server 4173, then open http://localhost:4173. No packages, build step, account, network connection, or downloaded fonts are required.

    You are East; turns proceed South, West, then North. The 136 physical tiles remain uniquely tracked in hands, exposed melds, discards, and a ron finish. A normal win is four melds (runs, triplets, or quads) plus a pair. East may tsumo on the opening hand; after that tsumo requires a wall or kan replacement draw.

    Ron outranks pon/kan, which outrank chii; ties go to the nearest eligible seat after the discarder. Only the next seat may chii. Passing an offered call resolves the remaining eligible claims. The hand ends at a win or exhaustive draw.

    Not included: scoring/payments, riichi, dora, seat/round wind scoring, closed/added kan, robbing a kan, flowers, special hands, or strategic computer call choices.
